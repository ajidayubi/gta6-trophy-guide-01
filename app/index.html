Leonida Platinum Roadmap

Time to Platinum: ~200 hours
Difficulty: 4/10
Playthroughs: 1 (Free roam available)
Online Trophies: None (100% offline)
Mission Replay: None
Missables: 1 (Positive Mental Attitude)

Stage 1: Story First (60–80 h)

Play through all 5 chapters with Jason and Lucia. Complete the Lucia balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (after "Down to Earth") — your only missable interaction. Keep manual saves. Scan vehicles with WAiNK and study animals with binoculars early.

Stage 2: 100% Completion (~100 h)

After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work through collectibles, stunt jumps, and activities. Leave the 8 large bank vaults until "That's Where The Money Is…" in Chapter 4.

Stage 3: Cleanup (10–20 h)

Finish Strangers & Freaks strands, character questline finals, remaining meditation spots, and outstanding combat or driving trophies. Unlock Vice Got a Price!

⚠️ Missable Trophy Warning

Positive Mental Attitude (Trophy #28): This is the only strictly missable trophy in GTA VI. Meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida; the first spot is the Lucia balcony meditation in Chapter 1, after the mission "Down to Earth" — find Lucia at Jason's house in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept when she mentions meditating; complete it before leaving Chapter 1, because there is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. All other trophies can be cleaned up in free roam after the story.
